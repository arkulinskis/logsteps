From a short walk to a room you can revisit

No special hardware, no tripod, no app to install. A minute or two of walking is enough for most rooms.

Step 01

Walk

Open Logsteps on your phone and walk slowly along the walls. It keeps the sharp frames and tells you when to slow down.

Step 02

We rebuild it

Our scan station runs lingbot-map, a 3D foundation model, on your frames, then levels the floor and cleans up the result.

Step 03

Step back in

Orbit it like a dollhouse, walk through at eye height, see the floor plan, replay your walk, measure anything.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Scanning, viewing, sharing and Studio are free.

Which phones work?

Any recent phone with a camera and a modern browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox). No app to install. You can also upload a video you already filmed, or import a PLY or GLB from another scanning app.

How accurate are the measurements?

Logsteps estimates the scale from how high you held your phone, which is usually within a few percent. For exact numbers, calibrate in Studio: click both ends of something you know (a door, a table) and type its real length.

How long does a scan take?

Usually one to a few minutes once the scan station picks it up. If many people are scanning at once, yours waits in a queue and the app shows where it is.

Who can see my rooms?

Only you, until you choose otherwise. A room can be private, shared with a link (anyone who has it can look), or public in Explore. You can change your mind or make a new link at any time. Privacy & Terms has the details.

What happens to my footage?

The frames are kept with the room so you can replay them and process the room again. You can delete the footage of any room and keep the 3D model.

What powers it?

The 3D reconstruction is lingbot-map by Robbyant (open source, Apache-2.0), a streaming 3D reconstruction model, running on our own GPU scan station.

Got a room nearby?

You probably do. It takes about a minute to scan it.
