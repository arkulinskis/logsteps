Four tips for a great scan
1Light it up

Open the curtains and switch the lights on. Dark corners come out patchy.

2Walk, don't spin

Walk slowly along the walls about a metre away. Turning on the spot gives the model nothing to measure.

3Sweep up and down

Tilt the phone to catch the floor and ceiling edges as you go.

4Close the loop

Finish where you started. It helps everything line up.
